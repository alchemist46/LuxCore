# LuxCore roadmap: the open work

Consolidated 2026-10-21 (HEAD `158a5b409`) from the unfinished items of [MODERNIZATION_PLAN.md](docs/history/MODERNIZATION_PLAN.md) and the whole of [SPEEDUP_CHANGES.md](docs/history/SPEEDUP_CHANGES.md). It lists open work only. The done items, their measurements and the reasoning behind them stay in those two files and in [MODERNIZATION_RESEARCH.md](docs/history/MODERNIZATION_RESEARCH.md) (papers, engine comparisons), all in `docs/history/`; each entry below says where to look (*History*). Bugs are in [BUGS.md](BUGS.md); an item that fixes one names it. Bugs with no item here are standalone fixes.

## 1. Where the engine stands

Against Cycles on the same RX 6950 XT (G0 re-measure of 2026-10-17, light tree build): **4.5x** at equal error on the Blender Cornell box, **8.6x** on the Blender classroom. The two factors, from the speedup plan:

| | Cornell box | classroom |
|---|---|---|
| time per sample, PATHOCL / Cycles | ~1.4x | ~3x |
| variance per sample, PATHOCL / Cycles | ~1.8x | ~0.85x |

The trace is not the limit (203M rays/s on the 32-triangle Cornell box, 187M on the 247k-triangle classroom). Textured scenes lose on the shading of a vertex; simple scenes lose on direct-light noise. The fixed cost of a render is 0.85-4.3 s against 0-0.3 s for Cycles. The cold kernel compile is solved on Windows (G9).

Done, for orientation: CI with tests and the benchmark set; S0 (the kernel timing and the textures' share); A3; B1 (the hit point diet; the lazy `dndu/dndv` measured and dropped); B3 (the learning stop); B4; E3; the texture half of G11b; Owen-scrambled Sobol with blue-noise pixel decorrelation; the per-pixel adaptive halt (as an option); GGX/VNDF and energy compensation always on; the material standards pass; random-walk subsurface; OpenPBR; the Arnold/Cycles path depths; the working colour space; the light tree as the default strategy; G1-G5, G7, G10, G11; G9 on Windows; C1-C5; F1-F3; P1-P3; RANDOM sampler removed.

## 2. Conventions

Entry: **ID. Title** `[tag]` — what, why, where in the code, how it is checked, dependencies, size. Tags: none = not started, `[PARTIAL]`, `[BLOCKED]`, `[MEASURE]` (measurement only), `[OPTION]` (implemented as an opt-in; the default is the open decision).

Measurement rules (from [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) and the speedup plan):
- GPU (PATHOCL on HIP) by default; CPU engines only when a test needs them. Short runs first.
- Noise without a reference: two seeds, `mean((a-b)^2)/2`, at 64 and 256 spp. A reference (~1024 spp) only for bias.
- **Bit-identical items:** TILEPATHOCL, fixed seed, `tilepath.sampling.aa.size` 8, no multipass; compare to 1e-7 against the previous build on cornell, luxball-hdr, classroom, kitchen, luxball-hdr-vol, cornell-vol, transparencyball, cornell-invis, the shadow-catcher cornell and luxball-shadowcatcher, plus the OpenCL device.
- **Statistical items:** seed pairs and equal means on the GPU.
- **Speed:** `tests/benchmark/renderbench.py`, old and new builds alternating, rays/s on Windows (±1%); the `[AdvancePaths_MK_RT] VGPRs / scratch` log line before and after. Per kernel: `tests/benchmark/kerneltiming.py` (`LUXRAYS_HIP_KERNEL_TIMING=1`); the G0 Blender classroom as a pyluxcore scene: `tests/benchmark/g0_export.py` (`--flat-textures` for the textures' share).
- **Tests:** `pyunittests/unittests.py --filter OCL` on the HIP device (known failure: `EnvironmentCamera` TILEPATHOCL at the threshold). Materials: `tests/furnace/`, `tests/bsdf/bsdftest`, CPU/GPU parity in each working space.
- After each commit: the BlendLuxCore check and the addon rebuild/install of `CLAUDE.md`; unresolved findings go to `BUGS.md`.

## 3. Tracks

### T1. GPU time per sample (PATHOCL)

Tiers A-C of the speedup plan plus the structural leftovers of the plan. Today: one `AdvancePaths_MK_RT` kernel per iteration, 256 VGPRs, ~2.5 KB scratch, 4 waves per SIMD; it is 86-99% of the GPU time, the HIPRT trace the rest (S0, 2026-10-21). On the G0 Blender classroom the image maps are 20% of the time per sample (31% of an iteration), and even without them a sample costs 5x the repo classroom's. Where a vertex spends its time: three interpreter walks per hit (volumes and pass-through), each texture evaluated 2-3 times per vertex, software bilinear image lookups, a 128-byte `Transform` copy per hit, N+1 wavefront iterations for a path of N vertices, repeated triangle-light work. *History:* SPEEDUP_CHANGES.md "Key facts".

- **A1. Evaluate each texture once per vertex** `[DROPPED]` for the image maps. Done: the bilinear footprint wrapped once per axis (-4.1% on the G0 classroom). With A2 every image map lookup done twice costs the G0 classroom +1.8% (415.3 -> 422.8 ns/sample, two rounds; +14% before A2), so a per-vertex cache of their values cannot pay for its private array. The material parameter record (Cycles' closure model, filled once per hit and shared by Evaluate, Sample and Albedo; the CPU twin is C6) is a different gain, the interpreter walks rather than the lookups: weigh it with Tier C. *History:* SPEEDUP_CHANGES "A1".
- **A2. Hardware texture objects on HIP and CUDA** `[PARTIAL]`. Done on HIP: two texture objects per image map (filtered, and point sampling for the single-texel readers: bombing and the image map bump) on a HIP array, their handles in the device's copy of the `ImageMap` descriptions; the raw pages are not uploaded when every image map has its textures (density grids and white wrap keep them: AMD reads a black border whatever its colour); `LUXRAYS_HIP_TEXTURES=0` turns it off. G0 Blender classroom -12% time per sample. Left: CUDA (`cuTexObjectCreate`, the same structure; no NVIDIA GPU here to test it); the white wrap through the hardware (a black border plus the weight of the outside texels). Prerequisite of F1. `src/luxrays/devices/hipdevice.cpp`, `deps/hipew`, `pathoclbaseoclthreadinit.cpp`, `imagemap_funcs.cl`, `imagemap_types.cl`; CUDA: `cudadevice.cpp`, `deps/cuew`.
- **A4. No-stack fast path for one-op materials** `[DROPPED]`. The private leaf stack on the global-stack kernel cost kitchen 27%; the register-argument form rewrites the stack interface of every material for the leaf materials of the Mix scenes only (the others already have G11's private stack). Revisit with Tier C. *History:* SPEEDUP_CHANGES "A4".
- **A5. Common subexpression sharing in the texture op compiler.** Memoize per texture index inside one evaluation (a slot area at the bottom of the texture stack, a `COPY_SLOT` op); `compiletextures.cpp:333+` flattens the graph with no sharing, the Blender classroom compiles to 7164 ops. Also shrinks the stacks (helps G11b). After A2 the image maps are 9.4% of the G0 classroom's time per sample (415.9 against 376.6 ns with the maps replaced by their mean colour, 2026-10-22); the rest of the op graph is not measured yet: first count the duplicated subtrees in `compiletextures.cpp` on the G0 classroom, then decide.
- **B2. No extra iteration at the end of a path** `[DROPPED]`. Measured 2026-10-22 with an upper-bound probe (the last shadow ray of a path dropped, so the sample is splat and the next camera ray made in the same iteration; `LUXRAYS_KERNEL_LOAD_DIR`, PATHOCL 64 spp, two rounds): cornell 29.1 -> 29.0 ns/sample (-0.5%), classroom 64.4 -> 61.9 (-3.9%), kitchen 69.7 -> 67.1 (-3.6%). The real item keeps that shadow ray and splats its contribution from a pending record a separate film add, which has to agree with variance clamping, the adaptive half buffers, the AOV channels and Metropolis (whole-sample splats), so it would recover less than the bound: not worth it. *History:* SPEEDUP_CHANGES "B2".
- **B5. Triangle light sampling without repeats** `[DROPPED]`. Measured 2026-10-22 with an upper-bound probe: the light pick pdf of `DirectHitFiniteLight()` (the MIS weight of an emitter hit, which recomputes the learned slot probabilities and walks the tree) replaced by a constant. PATHOCL 64 spp, two rounds: cornell 29.1, classroom 64.5, manylights 7.6 ns/sample unchanged, kitchen 69.6 -> 67.0 (-3.7%). Recovering kitchen's share means storing the pick's slot probabilities (up to 16 floats) in the task state at every vertex of every learning scene, which the classroom would pay for nothing; removing the private `learned[]` array of `LightTree_SlotProbabilities()` changed nothing (the compiler had it). The solid-angle repeats are only in the opt-in `trianglelight.solidangle` mode (MC). *History:* SPEEDUP_CHANGES "B5".
- **C. Occupancy re-assessment** (after A and B). *Re-run 2026-10-22 after B1 and A2 (PATHOCL 64 spp, two rounds): the kernel still sits at 256 VGPRs; `LUXRAYS_HIP_WAVES_PER_EU` 5 / 6 gives 192 / 160 VGPRs with more scratch and cornell 29.1 -> 34.4 / 31.7 ns/sample, classroom 64.5 -> 72.2 / 70-72, kitchen 69.7 -> 98.0 / 92.8: no free occupancy, what B1 removed was scratch, not live registers.* Re-run the `LUXRAYS_HIP_WAVES_PER_EU` sweep and the `-Rpass-analysis=kernel-resource-usage` dump (`hiprtc_remarks.py`). If the kernel then fits 5-6 waves without spilling, the gain is free. If not, the route left is the per-member SoA accessor refactor plus per-state kernels with queues: the old G6 (compaction), the G7 leftover and "material sorting" are this one item (Cycles' own tracker: compaction "not clearly a win" with few kernels per bounce). Multi-week; weigh it then, not now. *History:* PLAN G6, G7 ("rejected" paragraphs), G8 table.
- **G11b. Peak-depth bound of the material evaluation stack** `[PARTIAL]`. Done: the textures (peak depth of the op tree; the G0 Blender classroom 212 -> 21 floats, under the private 32). Left: the materials, sized by the sum of their ops (348 floats on the G0 classroom, 713 MB of shared stacks at 960x540). A peak bound needs the pops and pushes of every material op type (the ops pop their parameters; the sub-node model of the textures gives the sum again). A Mix of two leaf materials peaks at 34 floats and each nesting level adds about 28, so no Mix scene fits the private material stack (16): the gain is the shared buffer (taskCount x bound) and, with a private bound of 64-128 floats measured against the scratch it costs, the private variant for shallow Mix scenes, which also removes the recompile of a viewport Mix edit (1.7-3.1 s against the 15-70 ms kernel load). The addon should also call `RemoveUnusedMaterials()` so an edit back returns to the private variant. *History:* PLAN G11 "Left open" and G11b, G9 step 7.
- *Not pursued:* fused trace and shader execution reordering (OptiX-only, after the state shrink), per-scene kernel specialization (G4, measured and rejected), a private copy of the whole task state (G7, rejected).

What `.rsm` does today: `RenderConfig::serialize()` is empty so the scene and config were never in it (BUGS.md "Scene serialization crashes (.bcf, .rsm, .bsc)"); the RenderState holds only `bootStrapSeed` and the PhotonGI cache (plus the tile repository for TILEPATH); on resume the engine reseeds with `bootStrapSeed + 1` and adds the old film to a fresh one (`renderengine.cpp:125-128`, `pathcpu.cpp:91-111`, `pathocl.cpp:114-134`), so the sampler restarts at pass 0 with another seed and the join loses the Sobol stratification and the adaptive halt's per-pixel passes; the film serialization skips `maskObjectIDs` / `byObjectIDs` and writes `statsStartSampleTime` twice (`filmserialize.cpp:123-128, 233-238`). The checkpoint: (a) a multi-layer EXR with every film channel including the `_HALF` buffers, the convergence and noise buffers, the per-thread `FilmSamplesCounts` (`film.h:62-128`) and the Sobol per-pixel pass counters as channels; (b) a properties sidecar (or EXR attributes) with the engine tag, `bootStrapSeed`, the sampler shared state (`SobolSamplerSharedData`: `seedBase`, `bucketIndex`, `sobol.h:67-78`; GPU `sampler_types.cl:119-142`, uploaded at `pathoclbaseoclthreadinit.cpp:460-518`), the halt state, the stats and elapsed time, the radiance group count, the ID vectors, the working colour space and the outputs; the tile repository for TILEPATH as per-tile pass counts. The scene and config are not in it: the caller reloads its `.cfg` / `.scn`, which is what happens today anyway. The PhotonGI cache keeps its own `photongi.persistent.file`. Resume loads the buffers in place (no `AddFilm` merge) and restores the sampler counters, so the sequence continues. `periodicsave.resumerendering.period` and the `.flm` of `SaveFilm` write the same file; the API (`luxcore.h:1349, 1644`, `luxcoreimpl.cpp:504, 539, 1805-1819, 2170`, `pyluxcore.cpp:2045, 2629, 2684`, `luxcoreconsole.cpp:186, 260`) keeps its names with the new format. Removes most of the `boost::serialization` use that K2 wants gone (the film, the render states); the image pipeline plugins' `serialize` and `FilmOutputs` go with K2. Shares the named-channel EXR writer with F7. Check: 64 spp, checkpoint, resume for 64 more, against 128 spp uninterrupted: bit-identical with TILEPATHCPU and TILEPATHOCL at a fixed seed; equal means with PATHOCL (the task-to-pixel assignment differs); the halt stops at the same pass count; the `pyunittests` that use `.rsm`. - **X. XPU (CPU and GPU on one image).** After K1: the same integrator and shading code on both devices, so averaging cannot bias. ### T6. Features and interop

- **F1. Ray-cone texture LOD, stochastic filtering, UDIM.** One cone width per path (Akenine-Möller et al. 2021) selects the mip level per lookup; the level is sampled stochastically with one texel fetch (Pharr et al. 2024); one float of path state, the same code on CPU and GPU; fixes the load-time single-level choice at `imagemap.cpp:918`. UDIM tokens in the loader on top. After A2. Measure equal-spp RMSE and samples/s on a scene with large `.tx` textures.
- **F2. Out-of-core texture streaming.** OIIO `TextureSystem`, CPU only (no open-source path tracer streams textures on the GPU). After F1.
- **F3. Light linking and shadow linking.** MoonRay's model: a *light set* (the lights that illuminate the object) and a *shadow set* (the lights whose shadow rays the object blocks) per geometry, both "all lights" by default. Properties `scene.objects.<name>.lightset` and `.shadowset`, lists of light names (an emissive mesh counts as one light), resolved to `lightSceneIndex` at compile time and stored as a bitmask on the object (`sceneobject.h:96-102`, `sceneobject_types.cl:26-35`, parsed at `parseobjects.cpp:181-185`, with the two object copy paths at `:264` and `:331`, compiled at `compilesceneobjects.cpp:34-65`); 64 linkable lights per scene is enough, more is an error at first. No per-object light mask exists today: `lightgroup` is the radiance-group index and `visibility` is the indirect-hit mask. Three places apply the light set: (1) the pick, `DirectLightSampling` at `pathtracer.cpp:171-186` and `DirectLight_Illuminate` at `pathoclbase_funcs.cl:305`: the light tree gets a per-node union mask (`LightTreeNode`, `lighttree_types.cl:37-54`) and a masked traversal in `LightTree_SampleLights` / `SampleLightPdf` (`lighttree_funcs.cl:425, 540`; the CPU tree calls the same `ocl::LightTree_*` functions, so one change covers both devices); the 1D distribution strategies and the DLS cache reject unlinked picks and renormalize the pdf by the linked subset's weight sum, cached per distinct mask. (2) The emitter hit for MIS consistency, `DirectHitFiniteLight` / `DirectHitInfiniteLight` at `pathtracer.cpp:315-390` and `pathoclbase_funcs.cl:180, 227`: an emitter hit from a vertex on an unlinked object contributes nothing, so `EyePathInfo` (`pathinfo.h:93`, `pathinfo_types.cl:31`) needs the previous vertex's object. (3) The shadow set in the occlusion test next to the `cameraInvisible` skip, `scene.cpp:899` and `scene_funcs.cl:154`, with the shadow ray carrying its light index (the GPU already has it in `info`, `pathoclbase_funcs.cl:326-328`); `UpdateShadowBlockers()` (`scene.cpp:942-958`) must not count a shadow-linked object as a blocker, and the any-hit shadow fast path (`shadowRayAnyHit`, `pathoclbase.cpp:211-213`; the accelerators exit on the first hit, `bvh.cl:134`, `hiprtaccel.cl:100-116`) is off while any shadow set exists, which costs what a transparency scene costs today (V6's N-hit shadow rays restore it). BiDir, light tracing and PhotonGI apply both sets at the connection or the light-path hit (`Emit` callers: `pathtracer.cpp:1007`, `bidircputhread.cpp:633`, `tracephotonsthread.cpp:141`). Check: a scene with two lights and two objects each linked to one light: each object's pixels equal the single-light render within noise (seed pairs, both devices); a shadow set checked against the render without the blocker; `renderbench.py` for the any-hit loss. Addon: Blender's `Object.light_linking.receiver_collection` / `blocker_collection` (on the light) inverted into per-object sets at export (`export/light.py` and the object export); nothing exists in the addon today. 1-2 weeks.
- **F3b. Light filters.** MoonRay's light filters as a radiance multiplier on the non-intersectable lights: `scene.lights.<name>.filter.barndoor.{left,right,top,bottom,softness}` (edge angles in the light's local frame; spot, projection, point and sphere lights, the Z axis of `lightToWorld` as the direction), `.filter.cookie.{file,gain}` (an image map projected in the light's frame, through `imgMapCache` as the projection light at `parselights.cpp:390-396`, adding `IMAGEMAPS_EDIT` as at `:91-95`), `.filter.decay.{near.start,near.end,far.start,far.end}` (0 before the near start, 1 between, 0 after the far end, smoothstep ramps), `.filter.rod.{transformation,radius,edge,gain}` (a box region in which the light is scaled). Decay and rod depend on the receiving point and also apply to triangle and env lights later; barn door and cookie are direction-dependent. A `LightFilter` struct outside the param union in `NotIntersectableLightSource` (`light_types.cl:119-138`; CPU `light.h:155-192`), parsed with the common light properties at `parselights.cpp:464-476`, compiled with the common fields at `compilelights.cpp:359-366`. Applied in one place per device: the CPU `LightSource::Illuminate` becomes non-virtual and multiplies the per-type `IlluminateImpl` result, so the four callers (`pathtracer.cpp:184`, `bidircputhread.cpp:476`, `dlscacheimpl.cpp:215`, the DLSC build) do not change; the GPU after the switch in `Light_Illuminate` (`light_funcs.cl:1051-1152`). On light paths (`Emit`: `pathtracer.cpp:1007`, `bidircputhread.cpp:633`, PhotonGI) the direction-dependent filters apply at `Emit`, the point-dependent ones at the first hit of the light ray. A filter only scales radiance, so the pick pdfs and MIS stay valid; the tree and power strategies see the unfiltered power, so picks inside a decayed region are wasted until the decay far range feeds the tree's bounds (`lighttree.cpp:541`), a later refinement. Check: a filter at identity (no cut, gain 1) bit-identical to the unfiltered render (the TILEPATHOCL list); barn doors against a spot plus four black quads (means equal); CPU/GPU parity on one scene per filter type; PATH against BiDir for the `Emit` side. Addon: a filter panel on the light (`properties/light.py`, `ui/light.py`, `export/light.py`); Blender's Eevee `use_custom_distance` / `cutoff_distance` maps to the decay far end, the rest is LuxCore-only UI. About a week plus the addon.
- **F3c. The Prague/Nishita sky.**
- **F4. Geometry.** Deformation (vertex) motion blur; point clouds.
- **F5. USD Hydra render delegate.** The Hydra 2.0 scene-index API (default since OpenUSD 25.11; scene delegates are deprecated at runtime from 26.03), hdCycles as the template; Alembic and USD geometry come through the USD library. The biggest adoption lever (Houdini, Maya, Blender, Omniverse).

- **F7. Cryptomatte.** Film outputs `CRYPTOMATTE_OBJECT` and `CRYPTOMATTE_MATERIAL` in the standard layout (Psyop spec 1.2.0): per pixel N (hash, coverage) pairs sorted by coverage, N = 3 levels (6 floats, `film.outputs.<n>.levels`), the hash MurmurHash3_32 of the object or material *name*, and the EXR header attributes `cryptomatte/<id>/name`, `/hash = MurmurHash3_32`, `/conversion = uint32_to_float32`, `/manifest` (JSON name to hex hash). Today the ID channels are "last nearest wins" with no coverage (`filmaddsample.cpp:244-254`), the 24-bit IDs are packed into 8-bit RGB even for `.exr` (`filmoutput.cpp:565-599`), and the EXR writer takes one 3- or 4-channel `ImageBuf` with `chromaticities` as the only attribute (`:600-842, 846`): the output needs a named-multi-channel EXR writer (`CryptoObject00.R/G/B/A`, `CryptoObject01...`, plus the `CryptoObject.R/G/B` preview) and a manifest the film gets from the engine at init, since the film does not know the scene. Per-object and per-material name hashes compiled into the object (`sceneobject_types.cl:26-35`, `compilesceneobjects.cpp:34-65`) and the material, carried in the `SampleResult` at the first vertex like `objectID` (`pathtracer.cpp:600-601`, `pathoclbase_kernels_micro.cl:528-530`, `sampleresult.h:83-85`, `sampleresult_types.cl:39-41`). Accumulation in `AddSampleResultData` (`filmaddsample.cpp:222-267`, GPU `film_funcs.cl:280`): add the sample's filter weight to the slot with the same hash, else take a free slot, else replace the smallest if the new weight is larger; first version counts the first non-pass-through hit at full weight (transparent surfaces split their coverage later). The GPU slot update is a read-modify-write of a pixel several tasks can splat into: a per-pixel atomic CAS lock, cost measured with `renderbench.py`. New channel type in `film.h:143-192`, `filmchannels.cpp` (count, string), `film.cpp` `Resize` / `Clear` / `AddFilm` (a per-pixel slot merge, also what K5's resume needs), `CopyDynamicSettings`, the film serialization version, `filmparse.cpp:69` and `filmoutputs.cpp:60-370` (the `levels` property), `film_types.cl` flags and `FILM_PARAM` buffers, `compilefilm.cpp`, `ThreadFilm` (`pathoclbaseoclthreadfilm.cpp`), `luxcore.h` / `luxcoreimpl.cpp` / `pyluxcore.cpp` enums. Check: the per-pixel coverages sum to the alpha; the sum of one object's coverage over the image equals its `OBJECT_ID_MASK` count plus the edge pixels' shares; the EXR keys an object by name in Blender's compositor Cryptomatte node; CPU/GPU within noise. Addon: `view_layer.use_pass_cryptomatte_object/material` and `pass_cryptomatte_depth` in `export/aovs.py`, `engine.register_pass` for the `CryptoObject00..` passes and the manifest through `RenderResult.stamp_data_add_field` as Cycles does; nothing exists in the addon today. About a week plus 2-3 days of addon.
- **F8. Light path expression AOVs.** `film.outputs.<n>.type = LPE` with `.expression` in the OSL syntax (`C<RD>L`, `CS+DL`, `C<.D>.*L`; labels `L['name']` for a light and `O['name']` for an object), up to 8 expressions, each a float3 channel. The expression compiles to a DFA over the per-vertex alphabet (scatter D/G/S from the `BSDFEvent`, R/T, the object label) and the terminals L (light, with label), B (env light), O (emissive object); OSL's `lpexp` / `automata` files are BSD-3 and standalone (OSL is not in the deps, copy the two files), or a small own regex-to-DFA. Per-path state: one byte per expression in `EyePathInfo` (`pathinfo.h:93`, `pathinfo_types.cl:31`), advanced in `AddVertex` (`pathtracer.cpp:784`, `pathinfo_funcs.cl:76`, `pathoclbase_kernels_micro.cl:249`); at every contribution (`AddDirectLight` / `AddEmission`, `sampleresult.cpp:60-121`, `sampleresult_funcs.cl:89-176`, call sites `pathtracer.cpp:264, 357, 387`, `pathoclbase_funcs.cl:222, 278`, `pathoclbase_kernels_micro.cl:384-388`) the state is stepped by the terminal and the radiance added to the matching `SampleResult` entry. The GPU `SampleResult` sits in the task state, so the LPE entries exist only when LPE channels are on (the `SampleResult_Has*Channels` pattern, `sampleresult_funcs.cl:35-47`) and are capped at 4 there until measured; the DFA tables go in constant memory from `CompileFilm`. Film side as F7: the channel type, buffers, parse, output, HW buffers, enums. Second step: the fixed AOVs (`DIRECT_*`, `INDIRECT_*`, `EMISSION`, selected by `firstPathVertexEvent`) become built-in expressions, which removes that logic; light tracing and BiDir match the light subpath reversed at the connection. Check: on cornell and luxball-hdr with the deterministic TILEPATHOCL setup, `C<RD>L` plus `C<RG>L` plus `C<RS>L` plus their indirect forms equals the RGB channel to 1e-5 per pixel and each equals the matching fixed AOV; `L['g']` reproduces a radiance group; both devices. Addon: a list of named expressions on the view layer (`properties/view_layer.py`, `ui/view_layer_aovs.py`, `export/aovs.py`), each registered as a pass. 1-2 weeks.
